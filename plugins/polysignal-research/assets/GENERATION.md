# Icon provenance

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for PolySignal Research, then copied
unchanged to `assets/icon.png`. The publisher requested a signal-themed mark.

Prompt: Create a finished square app icon for PolySignal Research. A single bold
signal waveform: one continuous geometric line with two crisp measured peaks,
integrated with three small circular sample points. Centered, high contrast charcoal
symbol on solid warm ivory background. Minimal flat vector-like graphic with thick
strokes and ample padding, legible at 48 pixels. No text, no letters, no currency
marks, no arrows, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup. Square PNG image.
