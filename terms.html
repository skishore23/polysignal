The plugin provides offline arithmetic for explicit fee scenarios, supplied book depth, conditional complete-set bounds and hash-linked evidence. It does not place orders, hold funds, provide market access, verify current venue fee schedules, authenticate venue receipts or guarantee financial results. Its examples are synthetic. Calculated results depend on the input and stated model assumptions.

No subscription, purchase or payment is required by this plugin. Separate host software and services may have their own terms and costs. Local Python execution and a compatible agent host are required for the skills' executable workflows.
