01 / THE PROJECT

Research your next
market idea.

A research workspace for understanding how prediction markets behave—and how a trading idea might perform.

PolySignal collects public Polymarket buy and sell offers, looks for patterns, and tracks simulated trades through to their results. Its trading workflow runs in paper mode, so you can study ideas without placing real orders.

Want to start with your own data? The free PolySignal Research plugin brings fee calculations, available-order analysis, and research-record checks to your AI agent. It works offline, with no trading account or database setup.

positions together.

Estimate the minimum combined payout of paired YES and NO shares under a standard binary settlement. Compare it with your purchase costs and allowance for further costs.
