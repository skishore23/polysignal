01 / THE PROJECT

What remains
after the friction?

PolySignal is an open-source research system for understanding prediction-market microstructure.

It collects public order books, computes features, evaluates decisions with costs, and follows simulated orders through fills and outcomes. The repository’s execution path is hard-wired to paper mode.

PolySignal Research brings a focused part of that work into your agent: portable, offline tools for testing assumptions and inspecting evidence. No trading account or running database required.

THE RESEARCH CONTRACT

Keep the
unknowns.

01

Missing is not zero.

An absent exit leaves an interval of possible values. A known exit at zero is a different observation.

02

Depth is not a fill.

Liquidity in a snapshot does not establish what could actually execute after latency, fees or queue competition.

03

Integrity is not authenticity.

A consistent hash chain can detect edits against a trusted root. It cannot prove the venue, completeness, or truth of the inputs.
