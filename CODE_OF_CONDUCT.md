# Code of Conduct

PolySignal contributors are expected to keep collaboration respectful, technical, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect regardless of background, identity, or experience.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than individuals.
- Make assumptions and uncertainty explicit.
- Protect private data, credentials, and infrastructure details.
- Accept good-faith correction and help maintain a welcoming environment.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, credential disclosure, or retaliation are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub reporting tools. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
